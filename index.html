Kirka Hub

A free tracker for the browser game Kirka.io. Look up any player’s profile, their inventory and what it is worth, and what they have been trading.

  • Player search: profiles, inventories, loadouts and match history, with every skin valued.
  • Skins and prices: every skin in the game with its Hub value and a 3D render.
  • Trades: live trade offers and the full history of completed trades.
  • Daily leaderboard and ranked standings.
  • Clans: the clan registry and members.
  • Kirka chat, live lobbies, 3D fits and the Discord bot.
