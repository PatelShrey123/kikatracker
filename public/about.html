About Kirka Hub

Kirka Hub is a free tracker for the browser game Kirka.io. Type in a player’s short ID and see their profile, their inventory and roughly what it is worth.

What you can do here

  • Player search. A player’s profile, inventory, loadout and match history, with every skin valued.
  • Skins and prices. Every skin in the game with its Hub value, plus 3D renders of weapons and characters.
  • Trades. Live open trade offers and the full history of completed trades, searchable by player or skin.
  • Leaderboards and ranked. Today’s top players and the ranked Search and Destroy standings.
  • Clans. The clan registry, members and clan tracking.
  • Chat archive and live lobbies. Recent messages from the public global chat, and live matches you can join.
  • 3D fit and reload lab. A player’s loadout in 3D, and reload animations on real weapon models.
  • Discord bot. The same lookups inside Discord.

Where the data comes from

Player, clan, leaderboard and lobby data come from Kirka.io’s public API. Skin values come from the Kirka Hub valuation index, and trade history and some renders come from kirka.lukeskywalk.com. Values are estimates to help you trade, not guarantees; the terms say more.

Who makes it

Kirka Hub is built and run by Xpert (@tooexpert on Discord) for the Kirka community. It is free to use; the ads on the site pay for hosting. It is a fan project and is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Kirka.io.
