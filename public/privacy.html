Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Kirka Hub (kirkahub.online) is a free community tool for the browser game Kirka.io. It is not made by, affiliated with or endorsed by Kirka.io. This page explains what the site collects when you use it, and what the services it uses see.

What we collect

You do not need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address or password.

  • Searches. When you look up a player, clan or skin, the site asks Kirka.io’s public API for that data, sometimes through our own server so it works in every browser. The ID you searched for is sent to Kirka.io to get the answer.
  • Server logs. The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host it keeps short-lived technical logs of requests (the time, the page requested, your IP address and browser type) to keep the site running and to stop abuse.

Stored on your device

Some settings and cached game data are kept in your browser’s local storage so pages load faster and remember your choices, such as which cursor you use. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it.

The skin editor

The skin editor runs in your browser. Skins you paint or open from your computer are not uploaded; your current draft is kept in local storage so it survives a reload. If you load a skin by Minecraft username, that username is sent to mc-heads.net to fetch the skin.

The chat archive

The chat archive page shows messages that were posted in Kirka.io’s public in-game global chat. They are stored so recent chat can be read back, and messages older than 30 days are deleted automatically. Nothing you type on Kirka Hub is added to it.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to see how many people visit and which pages they use. These services may collect your browser type, device, approximate location from your IP address, and the pages you view. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this; Cloudflare Web Analytics does not.

Advertising and cookies

The site shows ads served by Google AdSense, which pays for hosting.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and to other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change that choice later.
  • You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalised advertising in Google’s Ads Settings.
  • Opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info, or in Europe at youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site still works, though ads may be less relevant.
  • Opt out of Google Analytics with Google’s browser add-on.

We do not sell personal information. Because there are no accounts, the only data we hold that could relate to you is in the short-lived server logs above. To ask about it, contact us.

Children

Kirka Hub is meant for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date at the top.

Contact
