Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Kirka Hub (kirkahub.online) is a free community tool for the game Kirka.io. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Kirka.io. This page explains what data is collected when you use the site.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account. When you search for a player, the site requests public profile data from the Kirka.io API. Some preferences and cached game data are stored in your browser (local storage) so the site loads faster; this stays on your device.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics and Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are used. These services may collect information such as your browser type, device, approximate location and pages visited.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

Kirka Hub is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us through the Kirka Hub Discord server.
