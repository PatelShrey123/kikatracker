The Kirka Hub bot brings everything on this site into Discord: look up players, value inventories, check skin prices, follow the in-game store and paint skins together live, all with slash commands. This guide covers VIP, the free live Skin Editor, linking your account and every command.

VIP

Kirka Hub is a VIP bot. In a server without VIP the bot only has /buy, which explains how to get it, and the free /skineditor. There are two kinds of VIP:

  • Server VIP unlocks every command below for everyone in one server.
  • Personal VIP lets you use the bot as your own app (“Add to my apps”) in DMs, group chats and any server, even ones without VIP. It is sold separately.

You can try /profile, /inventory and /link for free in the Kirka Hub support server. To buy VIP, join the support server from the bot page and message the owner.

The live Skin Editor (free everywhere)

/skineditor is free in every server, with or without VIP. It opens the Kirka Hub Skin Editor as a Discord Activity right in the channel, and nobody needs to be in a voice call.

  1. Type /skineditor, or open the Apps button next to the message box and pick Kirka Hub → Launch.
  2. The editor opens for you, and Discord posts a message in the channel showing it is running.
  3. Anyone in the channel clicks Join on that message to watch or paint along. Every stroke shows up for everyone at once, and people who join late see the skin as it is now.
  4. When the skin is done, use Download to save the PNG.

On the website, the Skin Editor’s Go live button does the same with a link you can share.

Adding the bot

Open the Discord bot page and use the invite button to add it to a server you manage. Once it has joined, type / in any channel to see its commands: just /buy and the free /skineditor until the server has VIP, then everything. /help shows the full guide inside Discord.

Linking your Kirka account

Linking tells the bot which Kirka account is yours, so commands like /profile and /inventory work without typing your ID every time.

  1. Type /link in Discord.
  2. The bot gives you a temporary 6-digit code.
  3. Paste that code once into the chat of a Kirka lobby in game.
  4. Click Done in Discord. The bot sees your code in the game chat and links your account.

The bot never asks for your Kirka password. If anything claiming to be Kirka Hub asks you for it, it is a scam. You can remove the link at any time with /unlink.

Players and clans

  • /profile: a player's profile card and stats.
  • /inventory: a player's inventory with every skin valued at its Hub Value.
  • /fit: a player's equipped character and weapons, as an image.
  • /clan: clan statistics and roster.
  • /leaderboard: top players or top clans.
  • /ranked: the top 10 players in a ranked mode.
  • /vip: Kirka Hub VIP members.
  • /h: set a custom background for your linked profile card.

Skins, prices and trading

  • /skin: price and details for any skin or item.
  • /highest: the highest-valued skin for a weapon or category.
  • /texture: the flat texture sheet of a skin.
  • /trade: live trade listings and completed trades, with visual cards.
  • /pricelog and /refreshprices: check the Hub valuation feed for price changes and post them to the changelog channel.
  • /newskins: skins that are in the game but not on the price list yet.
  • /unbox: simulate opening Kirka chests, just for fun.

The game right now

  • /chat: what Kirka's global chat is saying right now.
  • /servers: live matches and rooms across regions.
  • /store: the live in-game store, bundles and limited stock remaining.
  • /storeupdate: subscribe a channel to store drop and limited skin alerts.
  • /quests: active hourly, daily, weekly and event quests.
  • /events: event quests, clan war rewards and season rewards.
  • /weapon: weapon stats, or compare two weapons head to head.

Server and bot

  • /botname and /botavatar: rename the bot or change its picture in your server (server owner only).
  • /flip: flip a fair coin.
  • /suggest: send a feature idea to the Kirka Hub developers.
  • /support: join the Kirka Hub server for help and updates.
  • /donate: ways to support Kirka Hub's hosting.
