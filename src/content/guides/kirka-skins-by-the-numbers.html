Kirka.io's item list has 1,417 published weapon and character skins. Of those, 640 are owned by at least one player; most of the rest were added to the list during 2026 and nobody has one yet. Another 535 skins that players own are no longer in the published list, such as old rewards and special items. Every number below comes from Kirka's own item data, counted on 13 November 2026, and covers the 640 published skins players own.

"Typical" here is the median: half the skins in that tier have more owners, half have fewer. The jump between Epic and Legendary is huge. A typical Epic is owned by about 180,000 players, a typical Legendary by about 640. Mythical is the biggest tier, with 230 skins, and Paranormal is the smallest and the rarest: the typical Paranormal skin has fewer than 30 owners.

Surprisingly, the typical Rare skin has more owners than the typical Common one, so the rarity tier alone says little about how many copies of a skin exist. Check a skin's owner count instead.

The game added around 160 skins a year in its first three years, then very few in 2024 and 2025. 2026 brought a large new wave, and hundreds more skins are in the item list that no one owns yet.

The highest Hub Values

On the Kirka Hub price list, the most valuable skins are all valued at a billion coins or more:

  1. Rosy (Mythical VITA): 2.5 billion
  2. Gingerman (Legendary character): 2 billion
  3. Legend (Legendary VITA): 2 billion
  4. Love (Legendary VITA): 1.85 billion
  5. Gold (Legendary VITA): 1.55 billion
  6. Dissident (Legendary Shark): 1.5 billion

Four of the top five are VITA skins, and most of the list are Legendary rather than Mythical. Age, how many copies exist and demand matter more than the rarity tier. How Kirka skin values work explains why.

Values change as skins trade, so the live numbers are always on the price list and each skin's own page.
