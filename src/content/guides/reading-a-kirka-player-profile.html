Search any player by their 6-character short ID (for example #HESHPY) on the Kirka Hub home page and you get their full profile. This guide explains what each part of it means and how to use it.

Finding a player's short ID

Every Kirka.io account has a short ID made of six letters and numbers. You can see it on the player's in-game profile. On Kirka Hub you can type it with or without the #. A player's profile also has its own address, kirkahub.online/player/ followed by the ID, which you can share.

The header

The top of the profile shows the player's name, short ID and clan, along with a head picture taken from their character skin. Clicking the clan opens the clan's roster. Next to it is a quick summary with their Search & Destroy rating, K/D ratio and win rate.

Player stats

Combat

  • Games played, wins and win rate (wins ÷ games).
  • Kills, deaths and K/D ratio (kills ÷ deaths). A K/D above 1 means the player gets more kills than they die.
  • Headshots and headshot rate: the share of kills that were headshots, a good hint at aim.
  • Total score across all games.

Rating

The rating numbers are Kirka's own skill ratings: KLO for regular play and separate ratings for ranked, Search & Destroy, 1v1 and 2v2. A dash means the player has no rating in that mode yet. For where a player stands against the best, compare with the ranked leaderboards.

Account

  • Coins: Kirka's in-game currency, earned by playing.
  • Diamonds: the premium currency used in the store.
  • Total XP and XP to next level.

Loadout

The equipped section shows the character skin and weapon skins the player is using right now, each with its Hub Value. Open 3D Fit shows the whole loadout on the 3D model, the way other players see them in game.

Inventory valuation

The Inventory Valuation tab lists every skin the player owns with how many copies they have, and adds them up into an estimated net worth using Hub Values. Skins without a value yet (TBD) are not counted, so the real worth can be a bit higher. Share Inventory makes an image of the inventory to post.

Match history

The Match History tab shows the player's recent games, with the map, the result and how long each game lasted, plus their victories, defeats and win rate over those games. It shows how they have been playing lately rather than over their whole account.

Profiles in Discord

The Kirka Hub Discord bot shows the same profile with /profile and the inventory with /inventory. Link your account once and you can use them without typing your ID.
